Insider Buying Tracker

Open-market insider purchases from SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings

Corporate insiders — executives, officers, and board members — are required by the SEC to publicly disclose when they buy or sell shares of their own company. This tracker focuses exclusively on open-market purchases (transaction code "P"), the strongest publicly available signal of insider conviction. When an officer spends their own money buying shares on the open market, they are making a direct bet on their company's future.

Insider sales are noisy — executives sell for tax planning, diversification, estate planning, and hundreds of other personal reasons. Insider buys are different: there is almost no reason to buy your own company's stock on the open market except the belief that it is undervalued. This tool captures every such purchase filed with the SEC and ranks them by significance.

Each report covers a rolling 7-day window. The pipeline runs twice weekly (Tuesday and Friday) via GitHub Actions, pulling filings from SEC EDGAR, enriching them with company context from Polygon.io, and generating price charts with insider buy markers. Reports are published automatically to this page.
